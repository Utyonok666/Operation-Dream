Thank you for downloading my modular fence kit!

SETUP INSTRUCTIONS:
Please ensure your project is configured for the Universal Render Pipeline (URP), 
as all materials in this kit were custom-built specifically for this pipeline.

KIT CONTENTS:
The kit contains 4 modular units/prefabs:
  1. Wall with Connected Pillar (Main Unit)
  2. Separate Pillar
  3. Separate Wall
  4. Corner Unit

HOW TO USE & GRID SNAPPING:
1. Drag your chosen fence prefabs directly into your scene.
2. IMPORTANT: Set Unity's tool handle rotation toggle to "Local" (instead of "Global") in the top toolbar. This ensures your movement arrows always align perfectly with the fence's direction, even after making turns.
3. For seamless straight walls, set Unity's Grid Snap move increment to exactly 2.25 units (the combined length of the Wall + Pillar).
4. For clean corners and turns, set the Grid Snap rotation increment to 90 degrees.
5. Select a unit, press 'Ctrl + D' to duplicate it, and move or rotate it along the axis. (If global snapping is disabled in your toolbar, hold 'Ctrl' while transforming to force the snap!)

*Note: Snapping with the 2.25 increment is optimized primarily for the "Wall with Connected Pillar" unit, which is the foundational piece of the kit.*
